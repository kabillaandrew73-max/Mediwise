Amoxicillin

A simple guide to amoxicillin and the difference between bacterial and viral illness.

What is it?

Amoxicillin is a penicillin-class antibiotic used for susceptible bacterial infections when clinically indicated.

What it does not do

Antibiotics do not treat viral infections such as most common colds and influenza.

Safety

Allergic reactions can occur. Use antibiotics only when appropriate and as directed.
