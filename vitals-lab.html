Vital Signs Lab

MediWise educational resource.

Blood pressure

Learn measurement technique, repeated readings and the difference between an abnormal reading and a clinical diagnosis.

Pulse

Consider rate, rhythm, volume and context. Tachycardia or bradycardia should be interpreted with the patient’s circumstances.

Respiratory rate

Count accurately and interpret alongside work of breathing, oxygenation and overall condition.

Temperature

Interpret the number with symptoms, measurement method and clinical context.

Practice

Record a hypothetical set of observations and explain what you would verify before drawing conclusions.

Reference ranges vary by age, context, measurement method and clinical setting. Use your teaching materials and current local guidance.
