Measles

Measles is a highly contagious viral illness that can cause serious complications, especially in young children.

Educational information only. Symptoms can overlap with many conditions; diagnosis and treatment should be guided by an appropriately qualified health professional.

What is it?

Measles is a highly contagious viral illness that can cause serious complications, especially in young children.

Causes & risk factors

It spreads through respiratory droplets and airborne particles from infected people.

Signs & symptoms

Fever, cough, runny nose, red eyes and a characteristic rash are typical.

Diagnosis

Diagnosis is clinical plus laboratory confirmation where required, especially during outbreaks.

Management

Supportive care is used, with management of complications and vitamin A according to applicable clinical guidance.

Prevention

Vaccination is the main prevention strategy; reducing exposure during outbreaks also matters.

When to seek urgent care

Breathing difficulty, severe dehydration, seizures, altered consciousness or persistent severe illness require urgent care.

MediWise takeaway

Use symptoms as a reason to learn and seek appropriate assessment—not as a way to self-diagnose. Reliable health information should be checked against current guidance.
