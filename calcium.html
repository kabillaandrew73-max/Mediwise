Calcium

Student-friendly nutrition overview.

Why it matters

Bones, teeth, muscle and nerve function.

Food sources

Dairy, small fish with edible bones, fortified foods and some leafy greens.

Important note

Nutrient needs vary with age, sex, pregnancy, health status and other factors. Supplements are not automatically necessary just because a nutrient is important.

Source framework

Use current WHO nutrition resources and local dietary guidance for updated recommendations.
