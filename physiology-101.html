Physiology 101

Physiology asks a simple but powerful question: how does the body work?

Homeostasis

Homeostasis is the body’s ability to maintain relatively stable internal conditions despite external or internal changes.

Systems work together

The cardiovascular, respiratory, nervous, endocrine, renal and other systems continuously interact. A change in one system can influence others.

Study tip

For each body system, learn its major organs, primary functions, key regulatory mechanisms and what happens when normal function is disrupted.
