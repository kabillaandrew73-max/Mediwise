MediWise Med

Furosemide

Educational overview for students and the public. Not a prescribing guide.

Drug class

Loop diuretic

Common educational use: Oedema and fluid overload.

Safety points

  • Can cause fluid and electrolyte disturbances.
  • Use medicines according to qualified clinical advice, product information and applicable local guidance.
  • Do not share prescription medicines or assume a medicine suitable for one person is suitable for another.

Source framework

WHO medicines resources and relevant Ghana Health Service guidance should be checked for current recommendations.
