Overweight & Obesity

Overweight and obesity involve excess body fat that can affect health; they are influenced by multiple biological, environmental and social factors.

Educational information only. Symptoms can overlap with many conditions; diagnosis and treatment should be guided by an appropriately qualified health professional.

What is it?

Overweight and obesity involve excess body fat that can affect health; they are influenced by multiple biological, environmental and social factors.

Causes & risk factors

Energy balance, genetics, sleep, medications, food environments, activity and socioeconomic factors can all contribute.

Signs & symptoms

There may be no symptoms, but risk of conditions such as diabetes, cardiovascular disease and sleep-related problems can increase.

Diagnosis

Assessment can include body measurements, health history and evaluation of related risk factors.

Management

Management is individualized and may include nutrition, physical activity, sleep, behavioural support, medicines or procedures in selected people.

Prevention

Support balanced eating, regular physical activity, adequate sleep and healthier environments rather than relying on crash diets.

When to seek urgent care

Chest pain, severe breathlessness, fainting or other acute symptoms require urgent assessment.

MediWise takeaway

Use symptoms as a reason to learn and seek appropriate assessment—not as a way to self-diagnose. Reliable health information should be checked against current guidance.
