Pharmacology 101

Pharmacology is the study of drugs, their effects, mechanisms, uses and safety.

Pharmacodynamics

Pharmacodynamics focuses on what a drug does to the body, including targets, effects and dose-response relationships.

Pharmacokinetics

Pharmacokinetics focuses on what the body does to a drug: absorption, distribution, metabolism and excretion.

Study tip

When learning a medicine, connect its class, mechanism, indications, contraindications/cautions, adverse effects and important interactions.
