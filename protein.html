MediWise Nutrition

Protein

Protein supports growth, tissue repair and many normal body functions.

What is protein?

Proteins are made from amino acids and are used to build tissues and make many functional molecules.

Food sources

Sources include beans and other legumes, eggs, fish, meat, dairy, nuts and seeds.

Takeaway

Protein needs vary by age, health status and life stage; overall dietary pattern matters.
