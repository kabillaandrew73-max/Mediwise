Case 002 — Elevated Blood Pressure

Scenario

A 35-year-old has a markedly elevated blood-pressure reading during a routine visit but reports feeling well.

Think like a clinician

  1. How would you confirm the measurement?
  2. What history and risk factors matter?
  3. What symptoms would raise concern for acute organ injury?
  4. What further assessment might be appropriate?
Model reasoning

A single high reading does not by itself establish the complete clinical picture. Measurement quality, repeated readings and assessment for symptoms or acute target-organ problems are important.

Real patients require supervised assessment, appropriate investigations and locally applicable clinical guidance.
