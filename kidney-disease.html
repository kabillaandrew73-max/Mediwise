Kidney Health

Chronic Kidney Disease

Chronic kidney disease is persistent abnormality of kidney structure or function that can affect health over time.

Educational information only. Symptoms can overlap with many conditions; diagnosis and treatment should be guided by an appropriately qualified health professional.

What is it?

Chronic kidney disease is persistent abnormality of kidney structure or function that can affect health over time.

Causes & risk factors

Diabetes, high blood pressure and other kidney diseases are important causes or risk factors.

Signs & symptoms

Early disease may have no symptoms. Later, swelling, fatigue, changes in urination or nausea may occur.

Diagnosis

Blood and urine tests are commonly used to assess kidney function and protein loss.

Management

Management focuses on the cause, blood pressure and metabolic control, medication review and monitoring; advanced disease may require renal replacement therapy.

Prevention

Control blood pressure and diabetes, avoid unnecessary nephrotoxic medicines and attend recommended monitoring.

When to seek urgent care

Severe shortness of breath, confusion, very little urine, severe swelling or chest symptoms need urgent care.

MediWise takeaway

Use symptoms as a reason to learn and seek appropriate assessment—not as a way to self-diagnose. Reliable health information should be checked against current guidance.
