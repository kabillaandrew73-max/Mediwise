Pneumonia

Pneumonia is an infection or inflammation of the lung tissue that can make breathing difficult.

Learn • Verify • Empower
Why this matters: Understanding the basics can help people recognize risk, seek appropriate care and avoid common health misinformation.

1. What is it?

Pneumonia is an infection or inflammation of the lung tissue that can make breathing difficult.

2. Understanding it

It can be caused by bacteria, viruses and other organisms. Risk and severity vary with age, health status and cause.

3. Signs and symptoms

Cough, fever, chills, chest discomfort, rapid breathing and shortness of breath can occur.

4. Diagnosis

Diagnosis depends on the condition and may require clinical assessment, appropriate laboratory tests or imaging. A symptom alone should not automatically establish a diagnosis.

5. Treatment and management

Diagnosis is based on clinical assessment and may involve oxygen measurement, chest examination and imaging or tests depending on the situation.

Important: This is educational information, not a personal diagnosis or prescription. Treatment decisions should be based on appropriate clinical assessment and current local guidance.

6. Prevention and healthy habits

Treatment depends on the cause and severity. Antibiotics do not treat viral pneumonia. Severe illness may require hospital care.

7. MediWise Fact Check

“All pneumonia needs antibiotics.”

Vaccination where recommended, hand hygiene, reducing smoke exposure and timely care for respiratory illness can reduce risk.

FALSE — antibiotics treat susceptible bacterial infections, not viral infections.

8. MediWise takeaway

Breathing difficulty is a reason to take respiratory illness seriously.
