Clinical Skills

OSCE Practice Lab

Use structured checklists to rehearse communication, history taking and basic clinical skills.

Station 1

Hand hygiene

  1. Introduce yourself and confirm identity
  2. Explain what you are doing
  3. Perform the appropriate hand-hygiene sequence
  4. Maintain patient safety and dignity
Practice point: Know when hand hygiene is required and when gloves do not replace it.
Station 2

Focused history

  1. Open question
  2. Presenting complaint and history of present illness
  3. Relevant past history and medicines
  4. Allergies
  5. Social/family history when relevant
  6. Summarize and check understanding
Practice point: A good history is structured but conversational.
Station 3

Blood pressure

  1. Explain procedure
  2. Correct cuff size
  3. Position patient appropriately
  4. Measure correctly
  5. Repeat/confirm when indicated
  6. Communicate result safely
Practice point: Technique can change the number substantially.
Station 4

Respiratory assessment

  1. Observe general appearance
  2. Respiratory rate and effort
  3. Ask focused symptoms
  4. Auscultation if trained and supervised
  5. Identify red flags
Practice point: Escalate immediately when severe respiratory distress is present.
Station 5

Patient communication

  1. Introduce yourself
  2. Use plain language
  3. Check understanding
  4. Invite questions
  5. Respect privacy and autonomy
Practice point: Communication is a clinical skill, not an optional extra.
OSCE rule: Practice these stations under the supervision of qualified teaching staff when physical examination or clinical procedures are involved. This website cannot verify your technique.
