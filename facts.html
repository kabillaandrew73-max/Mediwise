MediWise Facts

Claim. Evidence. Verdict.

A growing fact-check collection designed to slow misinformation down and make evidence easier to understand.

FALSE

Drinking cold water causes pneumonia

Pneumonia is caused by infections or other lung processes; cold water itself does not cause pneumonia.

FALSE

Antibiotics cure viral infections

Antibiotics act against bacteria and do not treat viral infections.

TRUE

You can have hypertension without symptoms

Hypertension is often asymptomatic, which is why measurement matters.

NOT SUPPORTED

Blood group determines your ideal diet

There is not strong evidence that ABO blood type should dictate a specific diet.

FALSE

Natural means completely safe

Natural substances can have adverse effects, interactions and toxicity.

FALSE

More medicine means faster recovery

Taking more than recommended can cause harm; treatment should follow appropriate dosing and guidance.
