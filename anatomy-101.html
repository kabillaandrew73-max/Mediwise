Anatomy 101

A starter lesson introducing anatomy as the study of body structures and their relationships.

Learning goal

Understand the difference between anatomy and physiology and begin thinking in terms of body regions and systems.

Core idea

Anatomy describes structure; physiology describes function; pathophysiology examines altered function associated with disease.

Study tip

For each structure ask: Where is it? What is it connected to? What does it do?
