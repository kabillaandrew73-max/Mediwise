ECG Fundamentals

MediWise educational resource.

Start with the sequence

1. Rate

Estimate the ventricular rate and consider whether it is appropriate for the clinical context.

2. Rhythm

Ask whether the rhythm is regular or irregular and look for a consistent atrial-to-ventricular relationship.

3. P wave

Review atrial activity before examining conduction intervals.

4. PR interval

Assess AV conduction systematically.

5. QRS

Consider width and morphology.

6. ST/T

Look for repolarization abnormalities in clinical context.

ECG interpretation requires formal training and clinical correlation. Do not use this page to self-diagnose.
