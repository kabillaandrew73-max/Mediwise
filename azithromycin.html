Azithromycin

Azithromycin is a macrolide antibiotic used for selected bacterial infections when clinically appropriate.

What it does

Azithromycin inhibits bacterial protein synthesis. It does not treat viral illnesses such as the common cold or influenza.

Safe-use principles

  • Use only when prescribed or otherwise appropriately recommended.
  • Take the course as directed unless a qualified clinician tells you to change it.
  • Seek advice about allergies, drug interactions and significant side effects.

Why stewardship matters

Unnecessary antibiotic use contributes to antimicrobial resistance, making infections harder to treat.
