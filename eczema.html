Skin Health

Eczema

Eczema describes inflammatory skin conditions that commonly cause itchy, dry or irritated skin.

Educational information only. Symptoms can overlap with many conditions; diagnosis and treatment should be guided by an appropriately qualified health professional.

What is it?

Eczema describes inflammatory skin conditions that commonly cause itchy, dry or irritated skin.

Causes & risk factors

Genetic susceptibility, skin-barrier dysfunction, irritants, allergens and environmental factors can contribute.

Signs & symptoms

Dryness, itching, redness and recurrent patches are common.

Diagnosis

Diagnosis is usually clinical based on the skin appearance and history.

Management

Regular moisturization and trigger avoidance are foundational; anti-inflammatory treatments may be prescribed for flares.

Prevention

Use gentle skin care, moisturize regularly and identify personal triggers.

When to seek urgent care

Rapidly worsening redness, fever, significant pain or pus-filled lesions may indicate infection and need assessment.

MediWise takeaway

Use symptoms as a reason to learn and seek appropriate assessment—not as a way to self-diagnose. Reliable health information should be checked against current guidance.
