Macronutrient

Protein

Role: Growth, repair and many body functions

Protein is made of amino acids and is found in animal and plant foods.

Practical note: Needs vary with age, health, activity and life stage.

Macronutrient

Carbohydrates

Role: Major energy source

Carbohydrates include sugars, starches and fibre-containing foods.

Practical note: Quality and overall dietary pattern matter; not all carbohydrates are nutritionally equivalent.

Macronutrient

Fat

Role: Energy, cell membranes and absorption of fat-soluble vitamins

Dietary fats have important physiological roles.

Practical note: Focus on overall diet quality and favour healthier unsaturated fat sources.

Mineral

Iron

Role: Haemoglobin production and other functions

Iron is essential for oxygen transport and several enzymes.

Practical note: Deficiency can cause anaemia, but supplementation should be appropriate to the cause and need.

Vitamin

Vitamin D

Role: Bone health and calcium regulation

Vitamin D helps regulate calcium and supports bone health.

Practical note: Sun exposure, diet and supplementation needs vary; excessive supplementation can be harmful.

Vitamin

Folate

Role: DNA synthesis and red blood cell formation

Folate is important for cell division and blood formation.

Practical note: Needs are particularly important during pregnancy and should follow appropriate guidance.

WHO describes healthy diets using core principles including adequacy, balance, moderation and diversity.
