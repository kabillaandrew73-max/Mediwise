Hypertension

Hypertension is persistently elevated blood pressure. It often has no obvious symptoms, which is why routine blood-pressure measurement matters.

Learn • Verify • Empower
Why this matters: Understanding the basics can help people recognize risk, seek appropriate care and avoid common health misinformation.

1. What is it?

Hypertension is persistently elevated blood pressure. It often has no obvious symptoms, which is why routine blood-pressure measurement matters.

2. Understanding it

Blood pressure is the force of circulating blood against artery walls. Persistent elevation can increase the risk of cardiovascular and kidney disease and stroke.

3. Signs and symptoms

High blood pressure often causes no symptoms. Severe headache, chest pain, difficulty breathing, weakness, confusion or vision changes can require urgent assessment.

4. Diagnosis

Diagnosis depends on the condition and may require clinical assessment, appropriate laboratory tests or imaging. A symptom alone should not automatically establish a diagnosis.

5. Treatment and management

Diagnosis requires proper blood-pressure measurement and, where appropriate, repeated readings or additional assessment. A single reading should not automatically establish a diagnosis.

Important: This is educational information, not a personal diagnosis or prescription. Treatment decisions should be based on appropriate clinical assessment and current local guidance.

6. Prevention and healthy habits

Management can include lifestyle measures and prescribed medicines, depending on the person’s blood-pressure level and overall cardiovascular risk. Do not start or stop medicines based on an online article.

7. MediWise Fact Check

“If you feel fine, your blood pressure must be fine.”

Limit excess sodium, stay physically active, avoid tobacco, moderate alcohol where relevant, maintain a healthy weight, and attend recommended checks.

FALSE — hypertension is often asymptomatic.

8. MediWise takeaway

Know your numbers. Measure, confirm and manage blood pressure appropriately.
