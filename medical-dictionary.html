Medical Terminology Dictionary

MediWise educational resource.

Dyspnoea

Subjective difficulty or discomfort with breathing.

Tachycardia

A faster-than-expected heart rate; the relevant threshold depends on age and context.

Bradycardia

A slower-than-expected heart rate; interpretation depends on context.

Oedema

Abnormal accumulation of fluid in tissues, often producing swelling.

Cyanosis

Bluish discoloration that can be associated with increased deoxygenated haemoglobin; clinical assessment is required.

Prognosis

The expected course or outcome of a disease or condition.
