MediWise Policy

Medical Disclaimer

MediWise is an educational platform and is not a replacement for individual medical care.

Educational purpose

Content is intended to explain health concepts, medicines, nutrition and public health in accessible language. It is not a personal diagnosis, prescription or treatment plan.

Do not delay care

If you have severe, worsening or concerning symptoms, seek appropriate professional medical care. Emergencies should be treated as emergencies rather than managed from a website.

Medicines

Medicine pages are educational. Dosing, suitability, interactions and contraindications can depend on age, weight, pregnancy, kidney/liver function, other medicines and the clinical situation.

Editorial standard

MediWise aims to use reputable sources, distinguish evidence from opinion, and update content when reliable guidance changes.
