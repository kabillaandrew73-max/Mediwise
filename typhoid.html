Typhoid Fever

Typhoid fever is a systemic bacterial infection caused by Salmonella Typhi.

Educational information only. Symptoms can overlap with many conditions; diagnosis and treatment should be guided by an appropriately qualified health professional.

What is it?

Typhoid fever is a systemic bacterial infection caused by Salmonella Typhi.

Causes & risk factors

Transmission commonly occurs through food or water contaminated with feces from an infected person.

Signs & symptoms

Sustained fever, headache, weakness, abdominal discomfort and gastrointestinal symptoms may occur.

Diagnosis

Diagnosis may require laboratory testing; blood culture is an important diagnostic method where available.

Management

Treatment requires appropriate antibiotics selected according to current guidance and resistance patterns, plus supportive care.

Prevention

Safe water, sanitation, hand hygiene and food safety reduce transmission. Vaccination may be recommended in appropriate settings.

When to seek urgent care

Severe abdominal pain, confusion, persistent vomiting, dehydration, bleeding or deterioration need urgent care.

MediWise takeaway

Use symptoms as a reason to learn and seek appropriate assessment—not as a way to self-diagnose. Reliable health information should be checked against current guidance.
