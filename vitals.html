Vital Signs Lab

Learn the meaning, measurement principles and interpretation limits of the core vital signs.

Vital signWhat it tells youStudy interpretationCommon pitfalls
TemperatureBody temperatureInterpret in context of method, timing and illness.Method differences, recent activity, environment.
PulseHeart rate and rhythm at the measured siteAssess rate, regularity and character where appropriate.Exercise, anxiety, pain, stimulants and measurement technique.
Respiratory rateBreathing frequencyImportant marker of physiological stress; count rather than estimate when accuracy matters.Patient awareness can alter breathing.
Blood pressureArterial pressureRequires correct cuff size, positioning and technique; one reading is not always enough to characterize a pattern.Wrong cuff, talking, recent exertion, unsupported arm.
SpO₂Peripheral oxygen saturation estimateInterpret with clinical condition and device limitations.Poor perfusion, motion, nail products and device limitations.
