Iron

Iron is an essential mineral involved in haemoglobin and oxygen transport.

Why it matters

Iron is required for haemoglobin production and several other biological processes.

Low iron and anaemia

Iron deficiency can contribute to anaemia, but anaemia has multiple causes. Testing helps identify the cause.

Takeaway

Avoid assuming tiredness is iron deficiency or taking supplements unnecessarily.
