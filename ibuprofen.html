Ibuprofen

Ibuprofen is an NSAID used for pain, inflammation and fever in appropriate patients.

What it does

Ibuprofen reduces pain, fever and inflammation by inhibiting cyclooxygenase enzymes involved in prostaglandin production.

Important cautions

  • It can irritate the stomach and may increase gastrointestinal bleeding risk.
  • Kidney problems, dehydration, some heart conditions and certain medicines can make NSAIDs less suitable.
  • Use the correct product strength and follow the label or clinician/pharmacist instructions.

Key takeaway

A common painkiller is not automatically safe for everyone. The right medicine depends on the person, condition, dose and other medicines.
