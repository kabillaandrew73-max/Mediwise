MediWise Med

Medicine knowledge, responsibly explained.

Learn what common medicines are for, how they work at a high level and what safety questions matter.

Educational content only. Medicine choice, dosing and treatment changes require appropriate professional assessment and current local guidance.
Analgesic / antipyretic

Paracetamol

Common role: Pain and fever

Commonly used for pain and fever; safe use depends on dose and individual factors.

Safety: Avoid exceeding labelled or prescribed limits; liver toxicity can occur with overdose.

Penicillin antibiotic

Amoxicillin

Common role: Bacterial infections when clinically indicated

An antibiotic used for susceptible bacterial infections.

Safety: Antibiotics do not treat viral colds or flu; allergy and appropriate prescribing matter.

Antimalarial

Artemether–lumefantrine

Common role: Uncomplicated malaria when indicated

An artemisinin-based combination therapy used for malaria in appropriate settings.

Safety: Use current national malaria guidance; treatment choice depends on patient and disease factors.

Biguanide antidiabetic

Metformin

Common role: Type 2 diabetes in appropriate patients

Helps lower blood glucose and is commonly used in type 2 diabetes.

Safety: Use requires clinical assessment; gastrointestinal effects can occur and special situations need review.

Bronchodilator

Salbutamol

Common role: Relief of bronchospasm in asthma and other conditions

Relaxes airway smooth muscle and can improve airflow.

Safety: Frequent need for a reliever can indicate poor control and should prompt review.

Proton pump inhibitor

Omeprazole

Common role: Acid-related disorders

Reduces stomach acid production.

Safety: Long-term use should have an appropriate indication and clinical review.
