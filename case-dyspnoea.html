Case 003 — Shortness of Breath

Scenario

A patient reports new shortness of breath. The onset is unclear and the severity needs clarification.

Think like a clinician

  1. What questions distinguish acute from chronic symptoms?
  2. What respiratory and cardiovascular findings would you assess?
  3. Which red flags require urgent attention?
  4. What broad categories belong in the differential?
Model reasoning

Dyspnoea has respiratory, cardiovascular, metabolic and other causes. The first priority is severity and red-flag assessment, followed by focused history, examination and appropriate investigation.

Real patients require supervised assessment, appropriate investigations and locally applicable clinical guidance.
