Artemether–Lumefantrine

A high-level guide to an important antimalarial combination used for uncomplicated malaria in appropriate settings.

What is it?

Artemether–lumefantrine is an artemisinin-based combination therapy used for malaria when clinically indicated.

Why confirmation matters

Fever has many possible causes. Where available, malaria should be confirmed with parasite-based testing according to local guidance.

Safety

Treatment choice depends on factors such as age, weight, pregnancy, severity and current national guidance.
