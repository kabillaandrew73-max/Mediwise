Case 001 — Fever

Scenario

A 22-year-old presents with fever, headache, chills and weakness after several days of symptoms.

Think like a clinician

  1. What additional history would you take?
  2. What examination findings matter?
  3. What investigations could confirm or narrow the diagnosis?
  4. What danger signs would change urgency?
  5. What alternative diagnoses should remain on the list?
Model reasoning

Malaria is an important consideration in endemic settings, but fever has many possible causes. A careful history, examination and parasite-based testing where available help avoid assumption-based diagnosis.

Real patients require supervised assessment, appropriate investigations and locally applicable clinical guidance.
