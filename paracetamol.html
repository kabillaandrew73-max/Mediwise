Paracetamol

A high-level guide to paracetamol, its common uses and why dose matters.

What is it?

Paracetamol is an analgesic and antipyretic commonly used to relieve pain and reduce fever.

Safety

Taking too much can cause serious liver injury. Check active ingredients in combination products and follow the label or prescription.

Takeaway

Common medicines still require careful use. When unsure, ask a pharmacist or clinician.
