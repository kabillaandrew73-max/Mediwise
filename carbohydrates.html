Carbohydrates

Carbohydrates are a major source of energy and occur in grains, fruits, vegetables, legumes and many processed foods.

Not all carbohydrate foods are equal

Food quality matters. Whole grains, legumes, fruits and vegetables provide carbohydrates alongside fibre and other nutrients, while many highly processed foods can be high in free sugars or refined starches.

Healthy pattern

WHO describes healthy diets using principles including adequacy, balance, moderation and diversity.

Takeaway

The goal is not to fear carbohydrates. Focus on the overall dietary pattern, portions, food quality and individual needs.
