Case 001 — Fever in a high-transmission setting

Scenario: A young adult presents with fever, headache and weakness. The learner must identify key history questions, important examination findings, appropriate confirmation testing and danger signs.

Reasoning: Do not equate fever automatically with malaria. Build a differential and use appropriate testing.
